# Honmaru AI 1.1.0 (iOS)

The first release after 1.0.1. It carries everything built for the iPhone
since 2026-09-23 (PR #66 through #110).

## Before uploading

1. Deploy the Worker first. `main` is already deployed, so there is nothing
   to do unless the Worker changed since.
2. Build and upload from the Mac:
   `scripts/release.sh build 1.1.0 && scripts/release.sh testflight`.
   The Actions workflow (`testflight.yml`) cannot do this yet, because its
   signing secrets are not set.
3. The build uses push (`aps-environment`) and background notifications
   (`UIBackgroundModes: remote-notification`). The "HonmaruAI AppStore"
   profile has to include Push Notifications. If signing fails on the
   entitlement, reissue the profile (docs/push-notifications.md).
4. After it is live on the App Store, set nothing yet. Set `IOS_MIN_VERSION`
   on the Worker only when an old build has to stop working. The prompt to
   update comes from the App Store's own version, so it needs no setting.

## What's new (App Store text)

### 日本語

- チャットを大きく強化：スレッド、リアクション、ピン留め、「あとで」、書式（太字・リスト・引用）、メンションメニュー、@here / @channel
- 自分の言語で読める：メッセージ・通知・Activity を設定した言語に自動翻訳
- チームのエージェント：@名前で呼べる専用AI。調べもの・下書き・要約をスレッドで返します
- @AI に質問するとスレッドで答えます。決定カードは頼んだときだけ作られます
- 通知と既読がすべての端末で同期。どこかで読めば、iPhone の通知もバッジも消えます
- Activity は見たら既読に
- 新しいバージョンが出るとアプリ内でお知らせ
- 招待リンクは有効期間中なら何人でも参加可能
- そのほか多数の改善と修正

### English

- Much richer chat: threads, reactions, pins, Later, formatting (bold, lists, quotes), the @ menu, @here and @channel
- Read in your language: messages, notifications and Activity are translated into the language you set
- Team agents: AI teammates you call by @name, answering in the thread with research, drafts and summaries
- Ask @AI anything and it answers in the thread. A decision card is made only when you ask for one
- Notifications and read state sync across your devices. Read it anywhere and your iPhone clears its notifications and badge
- Activity clears as you look at it
- The app tells you when a new version is out
- Invite links let in anyone who has them, for as long as they are valid
- Many other improvements and fixes

## Update prompt (new in this release)

- On launch and whenever the app comes back to the foreground (at most every
  4 hours), the app compares its version with:
  - the App Store's own lookup for its bundle id, and
  - the Worker's `GET /app/ios`.
- **Newer version on the App Store:** a sheet with the version, the App
  Store's release notes, **Update now** and **Later**. Later brings the offer
  back after 3 days, or at once if an even newer version comes out. While an
  update is available, Profile shows an "Update to x.y" button.
- **Below `IOS_MIN_VERSION`:** a full-screen block with one way forward, to
  the App Store. It shows even when signed out.
- The offer waits until someone is signed in. It never lands on the
  onboarding screens.
